GHA’s role

GHA is a facilitator and coordinator of international medical education enquiries. GHA is not a hospital, healthcare provider, clinical training provider, university or accrediting body. Website information does not establish an institutional partnership or confirm that a programme or placement is available.

Observerships and hands-on training

Observerships are observational and do not grant patient-care duties, independent clinical activity, a licence to practise or professional qualifications. Hands-on clinical training is a separate activity and may occur only if the host institution expressly approves it and confirms applicable credentialing, authorisation, supervision, insurance and local regulatory requirements. The host institution controls clinical access, permitted activities, supervision and participant acceptance.
