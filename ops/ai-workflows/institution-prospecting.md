# Research guide — potential host institutions

## Purpose
Create an internal shortlist for human research. A listing is an unverified prospect, never a claimed partner.

## Workflow prompt
Using public institutional websites only, identify organisations that publish relevant medical education, observership or international visitor information in the selected destination and specialty. For each candidate, return institution name, country, relevant public programme page, published eligibility or application notes, source URL, date checked, and what remains unknown. Label every row `unverified prospect`. Do not infer willingness to host, acceptance, capacity, partnership or placement availability. Use institutional programme pages or general office contact pages only; do not harvest personal email addresses, staff biographies or private contact data. Do not draft or send outreach, fill a contact form, create accounts or add records to an external service. End with a short human verification checklist.

## Human review
A person verifies the institution and current page, checks that the programme is still active, and decides whether a separate outreach plan is appropriate. No message is sent from this workflow.
