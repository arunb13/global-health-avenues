# Research guide — medical education landscape

## Purpose
Prepare an internal, source-backed briefing on public medical education or observership information in a requested destination or specialty.

## Input
A destination, specialty, and a specific research question. Use only public sources. Do not provide private physician or patient information.

## Workflow prompt
Act as a careful research assistant for Global Health Avenues. Research the stated question using primary, official sources wherever possible, such as hospital education pages, regulator websites, government immigration guidance and published institutional policies. For every factual claim, give the page title, URL and date checked. Separate confirmed facts from interpretation and unknowns. Do not infer that an institution is a GHA partner, that it accepts international visitors, or that a placement is available unless the official source says so. For clinical access, licensing, visas, insurance or regulatory matters, summarize the official source and flag that the institution or a qualified adviser must confirm applicability. Return a concise briefing, source table, open questions and a confidence note. Do not contact anyone, submit forms, scrape personal contact information or store a profile of an individual.

## Human review
Confirm every linked source, remove any unsupported statement, and record the research date before using the briefing.
