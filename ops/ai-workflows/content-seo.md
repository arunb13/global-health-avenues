# Drafting guide — website content and SEO

## Purpose
Prepare proposed content and on-page SEO improvements consistent with GHA’s actual role.

## Workflow prompt
Review the requested page or topic and draft clear, accessible copy for Global Health Avenues. Describe GHA as a facilitation and coordination organisation. Keep clinical observerships clearly observational. Explain that hands-on training is distinct and requires express host approval, credentialing, supervision, insurance and compliance with local requirements. Never claim confirmed partners, available placements, guaranteed acceptance, accreditation, licensing, clinical practice privileges, outcomes or rankings without supplied, verifiable evidence. Provide a suggested title, meta description, heading structure, internal links and a short evidence list. Use plain language, avoid keyword stuffing, and flag any claim that needs human confirmation. Return a draft only; do not publish or edit the site.

## Human review
Check claims against written evidence, verify links, test reading order and contrast, and approve the copy before publishing.
