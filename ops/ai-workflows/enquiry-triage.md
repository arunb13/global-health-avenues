# Review guide — enquiry triage

## Purpose
Help a human organise a redacted enquiry for follow-up. This guide does not make participant decisions.

## Before use
A human must review the original submission and create a short summary that contains no name, email, phone number, registration number, document, patient detail or other sensitive information. Do not upload or paste an unredacted form response. If redaction is uncertain, do not use AI; review the enquiry directly.

## Workflow prompt
You are assisting a human coordinator at Global Health Avenues. Review only the redacted summary below. Categorise it as `physician observership enquiry`, `supervised-training question`, `institutional collaboration enquiry`, `other`, or `unclear`. Extract only non-identifying details needed for follow-up: specialty or area, destination preference, intended learning format, approximate timing if provided, and questions that remain unanswered. Identify whether the request mentions hands-on activity so a human can check for host approval, credentialing, supervision, insurance and local regulatory requirements. Do not assess an individual's eligibility, rank, accept, reject or match a person; do not promise a placement; do not invent facts; do not retain or forward the summary. Return a brief internal checklist and explicitly flag uncertainty. A human makes every decision and contacts the enquirer through the existing approved process.

## Human review
Use the output only as an internal aide. Do not copy it to an external system without following GHA’s privacy process. Delete the temporary redacted summary when the review is complete.
