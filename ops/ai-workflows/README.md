# GHA AI workflow guides

These are human-run, review-first prompt guides for internal GHA work. They are documentation, not deployed automations. They do not call APIs, connect to email or a CRM, send messages, collect records, or incur subscription costs.

## Shared operating rules

- Use public institutional information for research. Keep the source URL and date checked.
- Never claim a relationship, placement, accreditation, clinical access or programme availability without written confirmation from the host.
- Do not send email, direct messages, forms or other outreach. Produce internal notes only; a human decides whether and how to contact anyone.
- Do not enter patient information, health records, credentials, government identifiers, private contact data or other sensitive information into an AI tool.
- For enquiry triage, remove names, email addresses, phone numbers, registration IDs and any sensitive details before asking for help. If a record cannot be safely redacted, keep it out of the AI workflow.
- AI output is a draft for human review. It cannot approve a participant, make eligibility decisions, promise an opportunity or decide what clinical activities are permitted.
- Stop and ask a human when evidence is missing, contradictory or unclear.

## Guides

1. `research.md` — map public programme and regulatory information with citations.
2. `institution-prospecting.md` — build a source-backed list of potential host institutions; no outreach.
3. `content-seo.md` — draft consistent, evidence-based site content and metadata.
4. `enquiry-triage.md` — classify a redacted enquiry for human follow-up, without retaining the submitted record.

Before using any guide, check that the chosen AI service and its data settings are appropriate for the material being handled. No automated data transfer is configured by this repository.
