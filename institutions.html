How We Work

A Coordinated Approach to International Medical Education

Global Health Avenues acts as a facilitation and coordination partner, helping connect physician learning objectives with institutional education opportunities.

  • Understand the institution's educational priorities and capacity.
  • Identify suitable programme formats and specialty areas.
  • Coordinate communication and participant requirements.
  • Support programme planning and pre-arrival coordination.
Institution-led clinical governance

Host institutions retain responsibility for participant acceptance, clinical oversight, supervision, credentialing, permitted activities and applicable regulatory requirements. GHA does not independently grant clinical access or practice privileges.

A Clear Process

From Initial Discussion to Programme Planning

Each opportunity is explored in consultation with the relevant institution and is subject to feasibility and approval.

01 — Connect

Discuss institutional interests, capabilities and potential areas of collaboration.

02 — Define

Establish specialty, learning objectives, participant profile and programme format.

03 — Assess

Review institutional capacity, approvals, supervision and regulatory requirements.

04 — Coordinate

Where approved, coordinate participant preparation and programme arrangements.
