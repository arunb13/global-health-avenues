For healthcare institutions

Education shaped by your institution.

GHA works with hospitals and healthcare institutions to explore international medical education concepts that fit their educational priorities, capacity and governance.

Potential programme formats

Designed around host priorities.

These are formats for discussion, not advertised placements or confirmed institutional partnerships.

OBSERVATIONAL

Clinical observerships

Explore whether international physicians may observe approved clinical environments, teams and workflows within host-defined boundaries.

  • Host-controlled access and schedule
  • Clear learning objectives
  • Patient privacy protections
HOST-APPROVED PRACTICAL LEARNING

Supervised training

Consider only if your institution expressly approves specific practical activities and confirms oversight, credentialing, insurance and regulatory requirements.

  • Your clinical governance leads
  • Activity scope set by your institution
  • Local rules and insurance addressed
COHORT OR INDIVIDUAL

Custom education programmes

Discuss a defined programme concept for individual physicians, departments or cohorts when it aligns with host capacity.

  • Institution-led design
  • Participant profile agreed in advance
  • Feasibility before any commitment

Our facilitation role

We coordinate. You retain control.

Global Health Avenues can help clarify educational objectives, coordinate communication and support programme planning between institutions and physicians.

GHA is not a hospital, clinical training provider or accrediting body. We do not grant clinical access, approve participants or determine permissible clinical activity.

Host institution responsibilities

Your governance stays central.

  • Participant acceptance and eligibility
  • Programme scope, schedule and capacity
  • Clinical oversight and supervision
  • Credentialing and authorisation
  • Insurance and local regulatory compliance
  • Patient privacy and institutional policies
