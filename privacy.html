Website information

Privacy notice

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This notice explains how Global Health Avenues (GHA) handles information provided through this website and its physician and institutional enquiry forms. GHA acts as a facilitator for international medical education enquiries. It is not a hospital or clinical training provider.

Please do not submit patient information. The forms are for professional enquiries only. Do not include patient names, records, case details, passwords, government identifiers or other sensitive information.

Information collected through the forms

The physician form asks for a name, professional email, contact number, country of residence, medical qualification, current role, specialty, years of experience, programme interest, preferred destination and start timeline, learning objectives, and optional additional information. The institutional form asks for an institution name and location, contact person and designation, official email and contact number, institution type, available specialties, potential collaboration areas, estimated participant capacity, objectives, and optional additional information. Please provide only what is relevant to your enquiry.

How information is used

GHA uses enquiry information to understand and respond to your request, assess whether a potential education opportunity may be relevant, and coordinate follow-up where appropriate. GHA will not represent that an opportunity or placement is available unless a host institution has confirmed it.

Google Forms

The physician and institutional forms are hosted by Google Forms and embedded on this website. Information entered in a form is transmitted to Google and handled under Google’s terms and privacy practices as well as GHA’s use described here. The forms have explicit professional or official email fields; the current form settings display that a respondent’s signed-in Google account email is not part of the response. You may contact GHA by email instead. Google may process technical information required to operate its service.

Sharing and international enquiries

GHA does not sell enquiry information. If an enquiry may fit a potential institution, GHA will discuss any proposed sharing with you before sending identifiable information to that institution. Because enquiries relate to international education, any approved coordination may involve organisations in another country. You can ask questions or withdraw an enquiry at any time.

Retention and requests

GHA keeps enquiry information for as long as it is needed to respond and manage follow-up, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements. A fixed deletion schedule is not stated in this notice. Form responses may remain in GHA’s Google Forms account and any linked Google storage until removed. You may request access, correction or deletion by emailing globalhealthavenues@gmail.com. GHA will review requests in light of applicable obligations; Google may need to handle copies under its own controls.

Security and third-party services

GHA takes reasonable care in handling enquiries. This website does not currently use advertising trackers or analytics. The site uses externally hosted editorial images served from Unsplash image infrastructure; your browser makes a request to that provider to display them, subject to its terms and privacy practices. Embedded Google Forms are a third-party service and may use their own technical storage or cookies. No online transmission or storage method can be described as completely secure.

Children

This website and its enquiry forms are intended for adult medical professionals and healthcare institutions. They are not designed for children.

Updates and contact

GHA may update this notice as its services or information practices change. The current version will be posted on this page. For privacy questions or requests, contact globalhealthavenues@gmail.com.

For questions about how a specific enquiry is handled, contact GHA at globalhealthavenues@gmail.com.
