Your Professional Development

Learning From Healthcare Systems Around the World

Global Health Avenues facilitates connections between practising physicians and suitable international healthcare institutions. Our aim is to help doctors explore structured learning opportunities, gain exposure to different clinical environments and pursue professional development beyond their home healthcare systems.

Our Programmes

Two Pathways to Clinical Learning

Opportunities are explored according to specialty, experience, learning objectives and host institution approval.

01 / OBSERVATIONAL LEARNING

International Clinical Observerships

Structured opportunities to observe clinical teams, hospital workflows, specialist practices and healthcare delivery approaches in an international setting.

  • Exposure to specialist departments
  • Observation of clinical practices
  • Learning from experienced healthcare professionals
  • Understanding hospital protocols and workflows
  • Defined learning objectives and duration
02 / SUPERVISED PRACTICAL LEARNING

Hands-on Clinical Training

Practical clinical education opportunities developed with participating hospitals and delivered within approved supervision and authorisation arrangements.

  • Practical skills development
  • Specialty-focused learning
  • Supervised educational activities
  • Structured training objectives
  • Host institution oversight

The Process

From Enquiry to Learning Opportunity

A coordinated process designed to understand your needs and explore appropriate institutional opportunities.

01. Enquiry

Share your specialty, experience and learning objectives.

02. Assessment

Review your requirements and explore potential programme matches.

03. Coordination

Support applications and host institution approval.

04. Programme

Coordinate participation in the approved educational programme.

Getting Started

Information to Prepare

Providing the following information helps us understand your professional background and learning interests.

  • Medical qualification and country of registration
  • Current professional role and years of experience
  • Medical specialty or area of interest
  • Preferred destination country
  • Preferred programme type
  • Desired duration and approximate timing
  • Specific learning objectives
Programme eligibility, availability and participation requirements vary by host institution and destination. GHA will explore opportunities but cannot guarantee placement or acceptance.
