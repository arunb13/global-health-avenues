A considered starting point

Begin with what you want to learn.

GHA helps physicians explore potential connections with healthcare institutions. We start by understanding your specialty, professional background, preferred format and educational objectives.

We can discuss an opportunity only after a relevant host confirms it is feasible. Every host sets its own eligibility, documentation, participation and approval requirements.

Observerships are observational. They do not include patient-care duties or confer a licence to practise. Hands-on activity is a separate pathway and requires express host approval, credentialing, supervision, insurance and compliance with local rules.

What to consider

Good preparation makes the conversation useful.

  • Your current specialty and professional role
  • The clinical environment you hope to observe or learn in
  • Your preferred destination and approximate timing
  • Clear learning objectives and intended duration

Programme formats

Know the difference before you enquire.

Observational learning and hands-on training have distinct permissions and responsibilities. The host institution determines what it can offer.

OBSERVATIONAL LEARNING

Clinical observership

Observe clinical teams, departmental workflows and healthcare delivery within the boundaries set by the host. No independent patient care or clinical practice is permitted.

  • Observation and educational discussion
  • Host-defined access and duration
  • Patient privacy and institutional rules apply
PRACTICAL LEARNING · SEPARATE APPROVAL

Supervised hands-on training

May be considered only where an institution expressly approves the specific learning activities and confirms all local requirements are met.

  • Host approval and credentialing
  • Authorised direct supervision
  • Appropriate insurance and local compliance
INSTITUTION-LED

Custom programme

For individual physicians or cohorts when an institution has capacity and is willing to develop a defined education plan.

  • Host-led learning design
  • Feasibility and participant review
  • No guaranteed acceptance or placement
