About Global Health Avenues

We make the connections. Hosts lead the learning.

Global Health Avenues is a medical education facilitation and coordination organisation. We help physicians clarify their professional learning objectives and explore potential opportunities with healthcare institutions.

Our role is to support thoughtful introductions, programme planning and participant coordination. Host institutions decide whether to participate, approve programmes and determine all clinical activity and oversight.

Two distinct pathways

Learning formats with clear boundaries.

Observational experiences and practical training are different programme types. Each opportunity depends on host capacity, approvals and the participant’s eligibility.

01 · OBSERVATIONAL

International clinical observerships

Structured exposure to clinical environments, specialist teams, hospital workflows and approaches to care. Observerships are observational; they do not grant patient-care duties or practice privileges.

  • Defined learning objectives
  • Department and team observation
  • Host-set scope and duration
02 · HOST-APPROVED PRACTICAL LEARNING

Supervised hands-on training

Practical learning may be explored only when a host institution expressly approves the activity and confirms the required supervision and authorisation arrangements.

  • Host approval and credentialing
  • Direct supervision and insurance
  • Local regulatory compliance
03 · INSTITUTIONAL

Custom education programmes

Institutions can discuss cohort or individual programme concepts aligned to their educational priorities, capacity and governance requirements.

  • Institution-led programme design
  • Clear participant profile
  • Feasibility before commitment
Important: GHA is a facilitator, not a hospital, training provider or accrediting body. Programme availability and acceptance are never guaranteed.
