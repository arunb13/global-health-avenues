About GHA

Advancing Healthcare Through Global Medical Education

Global Health Avenues aims to connect medical professionals and healthcare institutions through international learning opportunities. We facilitate relationships, coordinate programme enquiries and work with suitable host institutions to support structured hospital-based educational experiences.

Our Programmes

Clinical Learning Beyond Borders

We focus on two forms of hospital-based medical education, subject to host institution approval and applicable professional requirements.

01 / OBSERVATIONAL LEARNING

International Clinical Observerships

Structured observational experiences that allow physicians to gain exposure to clinical environments, specialist teams, hospital protocols and approaches to patient care.

  • Hospital and department exposure
  • Clinical team observation
  • Defined learning objectives
  • Programme coordination
02 / SUPERVISED LEARNING

Hands-on Clinical Training

Practical educational opportunities designed with participating host institutions and delivered only within their approved supervision and authorisation arrangements.

  • Structured practical learning
  • Supervised clinical skills development
  • Specialty-focused learning objectives
  • Host institution approval and oversight

Supporting Physicians and Healthcare Institutions

Two complementary pathways to international medical education and institutional collaboration.

For Physicians

Explore international observerships and supervised clinical training opportunities suited to your specialty, experience and professional learning objectives.

For Healthcare Institutions

Explore collaboration with GHA to facilitate existing educational opportunities or develop customised training programmes for individual doctors and institutional cohorts.

How It Works

A Structured Approach to International Training

Each opportunity is subject to eligibility, availability and the approval requirements of the host institution.

01. Enquiry

Understand the physician's specialty, experience and learning objectives.

02. Matching

Explore suitable opportunities with relevant host institutions.

03. Coordination

Support the application and institutional approval process.

04. Programme

Coordinate the approved educational experience and follow-up.
